# The Ultimate Guide to Mainland Company Formation in Dubai

Setting up a mainland company in Dubai offers unmatched flexibility and access to the local UAE market. Registered with the Department of Economic Development (DED), a mainland business is licensed to trade freely both within the UAE and internationally.

## Why Choose a Mainland Setup?

1. **Trade Everywhere:** Unlike free zones, mainland companies have no geographical restrictions within the UAE.
2. **100% Foreign Ownership:** Recent legislative changes now allow foreign investors to own 100% of their mainland companies for a vast majority of commercial and industrial activities.
3. **Government Contracts:** Direct access to bid on lucrative government and semi-government tenders.
4. **No Currency Restrictions:** Freely repatriate your capital and profits.

## The Setup Process

1. **Determine the Business Activity:** Select from thousands of activities approved by the DED.
2. **Trade Name Registration:** Submit proposed names adhering to the UAE's strict naming conventions.
3. **Initial Approval:** Obtain preliminary approval to proceed with the setup.
4. **Draft the LSA or MOA:** Depending on the activity, draft the Memorandum of Association.
5. **Office Space (Ejari):** Secure a physical office space and obtain the Ejari certificate.
6. **Final License Issuance:** Submit all documents and pay the required fees to receive your trade license.

## Post-Incorporation

Once your license is issued, the journey doesn't stop. You will need to process your Establishment Card, investor visas, employee visas, and open a corporate bank account.
