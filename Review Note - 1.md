### The Story Behind Our Tagline

- **Seamless Setup:**

By taking complete ownership of filings, approvals, legal drafting, and PRO processes, we turn what is typically a bureaucratic trial into a fast, transparent, and effortless experience.
    
- **Sustained Success:**

#### 1. Professional Consultancies & Digital Services

- **Activities Included:** Management Consulting, IT & Software, Marketing/PR, HR, Graphic Design, Legal Advisory, Accounting.
    
      
    
- **Primary Cost Drivers:**
    
      
    - Flexi-desk / Virtual Office eligible (lowest Ejari rent).
        
          
        
    - Fast-track processing with zero external government approvals.
        
          
        
- **External Approvals Needed:** None (standard DET / Free Zone approval only).
    
      
    
- **Budget Level:** **Low**
    
      
    

#### 2. Commercial Trading, E-Commerce & Retail

- **Activities Included:** General Trading, Goods-Specific Trading (Garments, Electronics, Auto Parts), E-Commerce, Import/Export, Wholesale.
    
      
    
- **Primary Cost Drivers:**
    
      
    - Customs Code registration (~AED 1,000–1,500).
        
          
        
    - Foreign Trade Name fee (~AED 2,000 for non-Arabic names).
        
          
        
    - Warehouse or retail shop Ejari depending on inventory size.
        
          
        
- **External Approvals Needed:** Dubai Customs (post-issuance).
    
      
    
- **Budget Level:** **Moderate**
    
      
    

#### 3. Food, Beverage & Cloud Kitchens

- **Activities Included:** Restaurants, Cafes, Food Trucks, Cloud Kitchens, Catering, Foodstuff Trading.
    
      
    
- **Primary Cost Drivers:**
    
      
    - Layout blueprint approvals & physical site inspections.
        
          
        
    - Specialized shop/kitchen fit-out costs & grease trap installations.
        
          
        
    - Staff Occupational Health Screenings & Food Handler Permits.
        
          
        
- **External Approvals Needed:** Dubai Municipality (Food Safety Department).
    
      
    
- **Budget Level:** **High (Operational & Fit-Out heavy)**
    
      
    

#### 4. Healthcare, Beauty & Personal Care

- **Activities Included:** Medical Clinics, Pharmacies, Beauty Salons, Spas, Gyms, Fitness Studios.
    
      
    
- **Primary Cost Drivers:**
    
      
    - Degree attestations, professional exams, and licensed medical staff.
        
          
        
    - Strict spatial and equipment compliance rules for premises.
        
          
        
    - Specialized disposal and health department permits.
        
          
        
- **External Approvals Needed:** Dubai Health Authority (DHA) / Ministry of Health (MOH) / Health & Safety Department.
    
      
    
- **Budget Level:** **High**
    
      
    

#### 5. Contracting, Engineering & Real Estate

- **Activities Included:** General Building Contracting, Civil Works, Interior Fit-Out, Real Estate Brokerage, Property Management, Maintenance.
    
      
    
- **Primary Cost Drivers:**
    
      
    - Mandatory professional engineering exams & classification levels.
        
          
        
    - High labor quota requirements and MOHRE bank guarantees.
        
          
        
    - RERA broker registration and exam fees.
        
          
        
- **External Approvals Needed:** Dubai Municipality (Engineering Dept) / RERA (Real Estate Regulatory Authority).
    
      
    
- **Budget Level:** **High**
    
      
    

#### 6. Industrial, Manufacturing & Heavy Logistics

- **Activities Included:** Factories, Assembly Plants, Packaging, Material Processing, Freight Forwarding, Heavy Transport.
    
      
    
- **Primary Cost Drivers:**
    
      
    - Large operational footprint required (Industrial Plot or Commercial Warehouse Ejari).
        
          
        
    - High power/utility allocations and safety compliance.
        
          
        
- **External Approvals Needed:** UAE Civil Defense, Ministry of Industry and Advanced Technology (MoIAT), Environmental Authorities.
    
      
    
- **Budget Level:** **Very High**
    
      
    

#### 7. Financial Services, Crypto & Investment Holdings

- **Activities Included:** Crypto/Digital Assets, Investment Holdings, SPVs, Asset Management, Proprietary Trading, Payment Gateways.
    
      
    
- **Primary Cost Drivers:**
    
      
    - High minimum paid-up capital requirements.
        
          
        
    - Heavy Anti-Money Laundering (AML/CFT) compliance setup and audits.
        
          
        
    - Specialized legal structuring and escrow accounts.
        
          
        
- **External Approvals Needed:** VARA (Virtual Assets Regulatory Authority) / Central Bank of the UAE / DFSA / ADGM.
    
      
    
- **Budget Level:** **Highest Regulatory Tier**
    
      
    

#### 8. Tourism, Media & Event Management

- **Activities Included:** Travel Agencies, Tour Operators, Film/TV Production, Photography, Event Management, Publishing, Holiday Homes.
    
      
    
- **Primary Cost Drivers:**
    
      
    - Specialized activity permits and ticketing platform integrations.
        
          
        
    - Mandatory bank guarantees for inbound/outbound travel operators.
        
          
        
    - Filming location permits and media distribution clearances.
        
          
        
- **External Approvals Needed:** Department of Economy and Tourism (DET Tourism division) / UAE Media Council.
    
      
    
- **Budget Level:** **Moderate to High**
